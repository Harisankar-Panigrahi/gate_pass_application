# Enterprise Feature Catalog

This document details the functional capabilities of the Gate Pass Management System across its primary operational domains.

---

### Visitor Lifecycle & Gate Operations

**Purpose:**  
Replaces manual, insecure paper visitor books with an automated digital lifecycle that prevents unauthorized access, reduces gate wait times, and provides a complete audit trail.

**Capabilities:**  
- **Digital Guest Registration:** Quick registration at guard desks or self-service kiosks capturing visitor name, organization, contact number, government ID reference, and live photo.
- **Real-Time Host Approvals:** Automated mobile push notifications sent to host employees when their visitor arrives, enabling one-tap accept or decline decisions.
- **Optical QR Code Passes:** Dynamic digital passes with cryptographic nonces, validity timestamps, and anti-passback protections.
- **Pre-Registration & Invitations:** Employees can pre-register planned guests, issuing digital invitation credentials with QR codes for fast-track entry.
- **Recurring Contractor Passes:** Structured multi-day passes for long-term vendors and maintenance staff with automated validity expiration.

---

### Personnel & Employee Access

**Purpose:**  
Provides a unified mechanism for identifying, verifying, and tracking employees and on-site contractors across multiple facilities and access points.

**Capabilities:**  
- **Digital Employee Badges:** Virtual smart ID cards accessible via the native Android app, displaying verified employee details and optical barcodes.
- **Unified Check-In Scanner:** Single camera scan at the gate immediately distinguishes between permanent employees, registered contractors, and temporary visitors.
- **Employee Self-Service:** Portal for staff to view their visit history, manage active visitor invitations, and review profile details.
- **Automated Credential Onboarding:** Secure automated email dispatch providing temporary login credentials and mobile app setup instructions upon account creation.

---

### Perimeter Security & Emergency Response

**Purpose:**  
Gives facility security teams real-time control over facility boundaries, emergency evacuations, and suspicious activity detection.

**Capabilities:**  
- **Live Muster Roll & Occupancy:** Instant real-time headcount of all personnel and visitors currently inside facility perimeters, essential for fire drills and emergency evacuations.
- **Watchlist & Blacklist Screening:** Automated screening against blocked individuals, triggering immediate guard alerts and denying pass issuance upon match.
- **Perimeter Lockdown Protocol:** One-click emergency lockdown disabling active gate passes and restricting access to authorized security staff.
- **Forensic Security Timeline:** Chronological event reconstruction tracking every movement, scan, and manual override associated with an individual or pass.

---

### Material & Asset Transit Management

**Purpose:**  
Prevents unauthorized removal of tools, equipment, inventory, and IT assets while tracking the return of borrowed or serviced items.

**Capabilities:**  
- **Dual Pass Workflow:** Structured management for Returnable (tools, test equipment, loaner laptops) and Non-Returnable (scrap, consumed materials, outgoing shipments) assets.
- **Multi-Tier Authorization:** Tiered sign-offs involving Department Heads, Logistics Managers, and Gate Security before materials can exit the facility.
- **Overdue Return Alerts:** Automated notifications and dashboard flags when returnable materials are not brought back within the approved window.
- **Guard Transit Verification:** Verification checklist for gate security to confirm physical item serials and condition before approving transit.

---

### Executive Analytics & Compliance Reporting

**Purpose:**  
Provides facility directors and compliance officers with data-driven insights and verified audit documentation.

**Capabilities:**  
- **Real-Time Dashboards:** Visual representations of current facility occupancy, lane throughput, and open security alerts.
- **Peak Hour Analytics:** Analysis of entrance lane traffic patterns to optimize guard station staffing schedules.
- **Scheduled Automated Reports:** Daily, weekly, and monthly audit summaries delivered automatically via email in PDF and spreadsheet formats.
- **Searchable Historical Logs:** Multi-criteria search across historical records (by date range, gate location, host name, or visitor identity).
